# FIRST LEGO League Programs

Team 1073 is proud to host and support FIRST LEGO League (FLL) programs for younger students. These programs introduce elementary and middle school students to STEM through hands-on learning and teamwork.

## What is FIRST LEGO League?

FIRST LEGO League programs are pathways into FIRST Robotics for younger students. They emphasize:

- **Engineering** – Building and programming LEGO robots
- **Problem-Solving** – Tackling real-world challenges with creative solutions
- **Teamwork** – Collaborating to achieve goals
- **Gracious Professionalism®** – Respect, learning, and inclusive competition
- **Fun** – Engaging with robotics in an accessible, age-appropriate way

## FLL Jr. Explore Expo

### Program Details

- **Age Group:** Kindergarten to 4th grade
- **Team Size:** Up to 6 students per team
- **Focus:** Building simple machines and engineering solutions with LEGO bricks

### What Students Learn

- Engineering fundamentals
- Problem-solving and innovation
- Teamwork and communication
- Confidence in STEM fields

### Team 1073 Hosts

- **Annual Participants:** 36+ area teams
- **Role:** Team 1073 helps organize and mentor at the expo
- **Community Impact:** Introduces hundreds of young students to STEM annually

## FLL Tournament

### Program Details

- **Age Group:** 3rd to 6th grade
- **Team Size:** 10-15 students per team
- **Focus:** Engineering LEGO robots and demonstrating team values

### Competition Components

- **Robot Game** – LEGO robots perform specific tasks on a playing field
- **Team Values** – Evaluation of teamwork, communication, and innovation
- **Challenge** – Real-world problem-solving component

### Team 1073 Hosts

- **Annual Participants:** 24 teams
- **Role:** Organization and mentorship of participating teams
- **Mentorship:** FRC 1073 members actively mentor FLL teams

## Why FLL Matters

FLL programs:
- Introduce STEM to younger students
- Build confidence and interest in robotics
- Develop teamwork and leadership skills
- Create a pathway to FRC for high school students
- Inspire the next generation of engineers and innovators

```
FLL Jr. Explore (K-4th) 
       ↓
FLL Tournament (3-6th grade)
       ↓
FRC (High School)
```

Each program builds on the previous one, creating a clear pathway to competitive robotics.
