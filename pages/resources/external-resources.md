# External Resources for Roboticists

A curated collection of resources useful for FRC teams, students, and robotics enthusiasts.

## Official FIRST Resources

- **[FIRST Official Website](https://www.firstinspires.org/)** – Organization info, programs, and resources
- **[FRC Game & Season Info](https://www.firstinspires.org/robotics/frc/game-season-info)** – Current year game details and rules
- **[FRC Event Calendar & Results](https://www.firstinspires.org/robotics/frc/events)** – Find competitions near you
- **[FIRST Community](https://www.firstinspires.org/community)** – Connect with other teams and mentors
- **[FIRST Forums](https://www.first.wpi.edu/forums/)** – Discussion boards and Q&A

## Programming & Development

### Robot Framework & Libraries

- **[WPILib Documentation](https://docs.wpilib.org)** – Official FRC programming library (Java, C++, Python)
- **[WPI Robotics Laboratory](https://www.wpi.edu/academics/departments/robotics-engineering)** – Research and resources
- **[CTRE Electronics](https://www.ctre-phoenix.com/)** – Motor controllers and electrical components
- **[Rev Robotics](https://www.revrobotics.com/)** – Mechanical and electrical components

### Community & Learning

- **[Chief Delphi](https://www.chiefdelphi.com)** – Popular FRC discussion forum and resource library
- **[FRC 1690 Resources](https://github.com/Team1690)** – Open-source code and resources
- **[The Blue Alliance](https://www.thebluealliance.com)** – Competition data and analytics

## Engineering & Robotics

### Design & CAD

- **[Fusion 360 for Robotics](https://www.autodesk.com/campaigns/fusion-360-robotics)** – Free CAD software for students
- **[OnShape](https://www.onshape.com)** – Cloud-based CAD platform
- **[FreeCAD](https://www.freecadweb.org/)** – Open-source CAD software

### Mechanical Components

- **[McMaster-Carr](https://www.mcmaster.com/)** – Industrial supplies and components
- **[Online Metals](https://www.onlinemetals.com/)** – Metals and materials
- **[AndyMark](https://www.andymark.com/)** – FRC-specific components and kits

## STEM Education & Career Resources

### Educational Platforms

- **[Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/)** – Free online learning (math, physics, engineering)
- **[MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/)** – Free university-level courses
- **[Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/)** – Online courses from top universities
- **[edX](https://www.edx.org/)** – Free and paid university courses

### Career & Internships

- **[FIRST Scholarship Program](https://www.firstinspires.org/community/FIRST-Scholarships)** – College scholarships for FIRST participants
- **[Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/)** – Job and internship search
- **[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/)** – Professional networking

## Mechanical & Control Systems

### Motors & Actuators

- **[Falcon 500 Motor](https://www.vexrobotics.com/falcon500.html)** – Popular FRC motor
- **[SparkMax Motor Controller](https://www.revrobotics.com/rev-11-1205/)** – REV motor control
- **[Talon SRX](https://www.ctre-phoenix.com/Releases/Production/Talon%20SRX/)** – CTRE motor controller

### Pneumatics

- **[FRC Pneumatics](https://docs.wpilib.org/en/stable/docs/hardware/hardware-basics/pneumatics/index.html)** – WPILib pneumatics documentation
- **[McMaster Air Cylinders](https://www.mcmaster.com/air-cylinders/)** – Pneumatic components

## Vision & Sensors

- **[Limelight Vision](https://limelightvision.io/)** – Vision processing for FRC
- **[PhotonVision](https://photonvision.org/)** – Open-source vision software

## Safety & Standards

- **[OSHA Guidelines](https://www.osha.gov/)** – Workplace safety standards
- **[FRC Safety Resources](https://www.firstinspires.org/robotics/frc)** – Team safety information

## Software Tools

### Version Control

- **[GitHub](https://github.com/)** – Code repository and collaboration
- **[Git Documentation](https://git-scm.com/doc)** – Git version control guide

### Build Tools

- **[Gradle](https://gradle.org/)** – Build automation tool used by FRC teams
- **[Maven](https://maven.apache.org/)** – Java project management
