# Team 1073 Scouting App

Team 1073 has developed an electronic scouting system to help teams collect and analyze competition data.

## Our Scouting System

### What We've Built

Our team has created an **open-source electronic scouting system** available for any FRC team to use. The webscout project allows teams to:

- Collect real-time match data
- Analyze opponent capabilities
- Make data-driven strategy decisions
- Share insights with alliance partners

### How It Works

The scouting app captures detailed match data including:
- Robot capabilities (mobility, scoring, climbing, etc.)
- Match performance metrics
- Consistency and reliability observations
- Strategic insights

## Strategy Subgroup

Our Strategy subgroup uses advanced data collection and analysis to:

- Scout opponents before competitions
- Identify team strengths and weaknesses
- Optimize alliance selections
- Develop match-by-match strategies
- Make real-time adjustments during competitions

## Open Source

We believe in sharing knowledge with the robotics community. Our scouting system is available for other teams to use and improve.

**[Access webscout on GitHub](https://github.com/FRCTeam1073-TheForceTeam)**

## Data-Driven Excellence

Using analytics and scouting data, Team 1073 has:
- Made better alliance selections
- Optimized robot strategies
- Improved competition results
- Helped partners make informed decisions
