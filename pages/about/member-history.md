# Team 1073 Member History

Team 1073 has been building robots and inspiring students since 2004. Our team photos document nearly two decades of roboticists and innovators.

## Team Photos by Year

We maintain a visual record of our team members for nearly 20 years:

- **2023-2024** – Current season
- **2022-2023** through **2018-2019** – Documented with team photos
- **2017-2018** through **2013-2014** – Complete photo archive
- **2011-2012** through **2004-2005** – Historical team records

### Note on COVID Years

- **2020-2021** – No picture available due to COVID-19
- **2019-2020** – Limited documentation during pandemic

## Our Legacy

Since 2004, Team 1073 has built a strong tradition of:
- Innovative engineering
- Student leadership development
- Mentorship and community engagement
- Competitive excellence

## Alumni Network

Many of our alumni continue to be involved with Team 1073 through mentorship, volunteering, and our annual **Alumni Sponsor Night**—an invitation-only event where current team members interact with alumni who share their experiences and stories.
