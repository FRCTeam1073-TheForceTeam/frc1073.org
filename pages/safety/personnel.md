# Personnel Safety Guidelines

Team 1073 is committed to creating a safe, inclusive, and respectful environment for all students, mentors, and volunteers.

## Student Safety

### Team Handbook & Training

- All students must sign the team handbook (LEADS-approved)
- Students receive yearly safety presentations and safety-related activities
- The Integration subgroup teaches safety knowledge development
- Safety training covers workplace hazards, equipment operation, and emergency procedures

### Attendance & Participation

- Attendance tracking at all-hands meetings
- Poor attendance affects competition eligibility
- Regular participation ensures students understand and internalize safety culture

## Mentor Responsibilities

### Requirements
- All mentors must be **school-cleared** before joining the team
- Mentors must respect physical and emotional boundaries of students
- Mentors practice and teach **Gracious Professionalism®**

### Conduct Standards
- **Substance use strictly prohibited** – No alcohol, tobacco, or drugs
- Mentors guide rather than make decisions for students
- Mentors assist with dangerous tools to prevent student injury
- Create a supportive, inclusive learning environment

## Team Culture & Values

### Gracious Professionalism®
- Treat all members with respect
- Support learning and growth
- Celebrate diverse backgrounds and perspectives
- Maintain professionalism in competition and community

### Inclusion & Belonging
- All students welcome regardless of background or experience
- Zero-tolerance for harassment or discrimination
- Mentors actively build inclusive team culture

## Incident Reporting

If you experience or witness unsafe behavior or an unsafe condition:

1. **Report immediately** to a mentor or team leader
2. **Document the issue** – What happened and when
3. **Follow up** to ensure it's resolved

All reports are taken seriously and handled appropriately.

## Team Handbook

All students receive and review the team handbook which covers:
- Code of conduct
- Safety protocols
- Attendance expectations
- Communication standards
- Disciplinary procedures

## Support Resources

Team members who need support for any reason should reach out to:
- Team leadership
- School counselors
- Mental health resources (as appropriate)
