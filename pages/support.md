# Support FRC Team 1073

Your support enables Team 1073 to compete at the highest levels of FIRST Robotics and inspire young people to pursue science, technology, engineering, and mathematics careers.

## About Our Funding

As a non-profit team, we rely on community support to cover:

- **Competition Costs** – Entry fees, travel, and accommodations for district and regional events
- **Equipment & Materials** – Robot components, tools, and fabrication supplies
- **Mentorship** – Professional mentors and industry partnerships
- **Outreach** – Community programs and STEM education initiatives
- **Professional Development** – Training and conference participation

## Impact of Your Support

With community support, Team 1073 has achieved:

- **Top 4% Global Ranking** – Our 2026 robot "Crashout" ranked in the top 4% worldwide
- **Quality Award** – Recognition for robustness and superior workmanship
- **FIRST Leadership Award** – Team members recognized for exemplary leadership
- **District Championship Qualification** – Advancing to the New England District Championship

**Every donation directly impacts our ability to compete and inspire the next generation of STEM leaders.**
