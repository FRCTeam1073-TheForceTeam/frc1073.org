# Donate to Team 1073

Your donation directly supports FRC Team 1073's mission to inspire young people to become leaders, innovators, and technologically-literate citizens.

## Why Donate?

Every dollar supports:

- **Competition Costs** – Entry fees, travel, and accommodations for district and regional events
- **Equipment & Materials** – Robot components, tools, and fabrication supplies
- **Student Development** – Training, mentorship, and professional development
- **Community Outreach** – STEM education programs and community demonstrations
- **Scholarships** – Support for students with financial need
