# Team 1073 Safety Policies

Safety is our highest priority. All team members, mentors, and volunteers are expected to follow strict safety guidelines and protocols during team meetings, fabrication, and competition.

## Safety Culture

At Team 1073, we believe that a strong safety culture is essential to:

- Protecting our team members' health and well-being
- Ensuring our equipment operates reliably
- Demonstrating professional engineering practices
- Creating a positive team environment

## Safety Guidelines

All team members must follow these guidelines:

### Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

- Safety glasses required in fabrication areas
- Closed-toe shoes in the workshop
- Hearing protection during noisy operations
- Appropriate clothing (no loose jewelry or oversized clothing)

### Equipment Operation

- Only operate equipment after authorized training
- Never operate equipment alone
- Follow all manufacturer guidelines and posted procedures
- Report broken or unsafe equipment immediately

### Workspace

- Keep work areas clean and organized
- Maintain clear emergency exits
- Report hazards or unsafe conditions
- Practice proper lifting and ergonomics

### Power Tools & Machinery

- Unplug tools when not in use
- Never modify safety guards
- Keep hands clear of moving parts
- Ask mentors for training before operating new equipment

## Safety Training

New team members receive comprehensive safety training covering:

- Fabrication shop safety
- Electrical safety
- Tool operation
- Emergency procedures
- First aid basics

## Emergency Protocols

All team members know the location and use of:

- First aid kits
- Emergency exits
- Emergency contact procedures
- Fire extinguishers

## Reporting Safety Issues

If you observe an unsafe condition or unsafe behavior:

1. **Stop the activity immediately**
2. **Notify a mentor or team leader**
3. **Document the issue**
4. **Follow up to ensure it's resolved**

## For More Information

Team 1073 is committed to maintaining the highest safety standards. For detailed safety information, specific guidelines, or to report a concern, please [contact our team leadership](/contact).

**Safety is everyone's responsibility. Together, we create a safe environment for learning and innovation.**
