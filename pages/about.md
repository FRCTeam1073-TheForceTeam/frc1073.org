# About FRC Team 1073

## Our Story

Team 1073, "The Force Team," is based at Hollis-Brookline High School in New Hampshire. We're dedicated to inspiring the next generation of STEM leaders through robotics competition and hands-on learning.

## FIRST Robotics

FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) was founded by inventor Dean Kamen to inspire young people's interest and participation in science and engineering.

## Team Structure

Our team is organized into four specialized groups:

- **Electromechanical** – Robot design, fabrication, and mechanical systems
- **Software** – Programming, automation, and autonomous systems
- **Business** – Strategy, partnerships, and outreach
- **Strategy** – Game analysis and competition planning

## Awards & Recognition

Our team has earned numerous awards and recognition for both technical excellence and community engagement.
