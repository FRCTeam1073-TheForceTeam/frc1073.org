# Electromechanical Subgroup

The Electromechanical subgroup is responsible for designing, fabricating, and maintaining all mechanical systems and structures of the robot. This is where engineering dreams become steel and carbon fiber reality.

## What We Do

### Robot Design
- CAD modeling and 3D design using Fusion 360
- Mechanical simulation and stress analysis
- Concept evaluation and prototyping
- Design reviews and optimization

### Fabrication
- CNC machining and precision cutting
- Welding and metal work
- 3D printing and composite materials
- Assembly and integration
- Quality control and testing

### Systems Integration
- Mechanical/electrical integration
- Subsystem mounting and packaging
- Cable and component routing
- Maintenance and repair

### Testing & Validation
- Mechanical load testing
- Component durability evaluation
- System troubleshooting
- Iterative improvements

## Skills You'll Develop

- **CAD & Design** – Fusion 360, design thinking, problem-solving
- **Machining & Manufacturing** – CNC, mill, lathe, welding operation
- **Materials Science** – Understanding metals, composites, plastics
- **Project Management** – Planning, timelines, collaboration
- **Troubleshooting** – Diagnostic thinking, iterative improvement
- **Teamwork** – Cross-functional collaboration
- **Safety** – Shop safety, tool operation, equipment handling

## Responsibilities

### During Build Season
- Design robot mechanisms and subsystems
- Fabricate parts using shop equipment
- Assemble and integrate components
- Test and validate mechanical systems
- Iterate based on testing and feedback
- Document design decisions and changes

### During Competition Season
- Support pit operations
- Perform maintenance and repairs
- Troubleshoot mechanical issues
- Collect data on component performance

### Off-Season
- Maintain shop equipment
- Organize and inventory materials
- Mentor new members
- Plan for next season
