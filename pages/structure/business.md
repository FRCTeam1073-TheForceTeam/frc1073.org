# Business Subgroup

The Business subgroup manages the business operations of Team 1073, including sponsorships, fundraising, community partnerships, marketing, and team logistics. We keep the team funded, connected, and in the community spotlight.

## What We Do

### Sponsorships & Fundraising
- Identify and approach potential sponsors
- Develop sponsorship packages at multiple levels
- Manage sponsor relationships
- Track donations and contributions
- Plan and execute fundraising campaigns
- Write grant proposals

### Marketing & Communications
- Manage social media accounts
- Create marketing materials
- Develop team branding and identity
- Write press releases and news updates
- Create team newsletter
- Design promotional materials

### Community Partnerships
- Build relationships with local organizations
- Organize outreach events
- Plan STEM education programs
- Host FLL competitions and expos
- Coordinate alumni engagement
- Represent team at community events

### Team Operations
- Manage team budget and expenses
- Coordinate logistics (travel, accommodations)
- Plan team events and meetings
- Handle equipment ordering and inventory
- Organize team communications
- Manage team website and online presence

### External Relations
- Attend business networking events
- Present team to potential sponsors
- Represent team at community events
- Build school and district relationships
- Connect with industry partners

## Skills You'll Develop

- **Business Skills** – Budgeting, sponsorship development, project management
- **Marketing** – Social media, branding, promotional strategy
- **Communication** – Writing, presentation, negotiation
- **Relationship Building** – Networking, partnership development
- **Leadership** – Team coordination, decision-making
- **Problem-Solving** – Creative solutions, resource management
- **Entrepreneurship** – Business thinking, innovation
- **Organization** – Planning, coordination, attention to detail

### How to Get Started

1. **Identify your interest** – Sponsorships, marketing, events, operations?
2. **Attend a team meeting** – Meet the Business subgroup

4. **Start with small projects** – Social media, event planning, merchandise
5. **Advance to larger initiatives** – Sponsorship development, fundraising
